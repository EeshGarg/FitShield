                      Privacy & about

                      Local-first. No accounts, no cloud, no sync, no telemetry, no analytics. FitShield does not upload your browsing history, and never logs the sites or apps you open.

                      On Android, FitShield blocks in two independent ways: a local VPN that resets connections to blocked sites by the name in the connection (TLS SNI) — no traffic tunnelling to any server, no HTTPS inspection, no certificates, no content inspection, and DNS is never intercepted or changed; and an optional app blocker that reads only which app is in the foreground (never screen content) to show a mindful pause.

                      • This Android build is preview quality. Site blocking works alongside encrypted / Private DNS. There is no custom block page for HTTPS sites (that would require interception FitShield won't do) — blocked sites simply fail to connect.
                      • Timing, and country/category selections above are saved on device; wiring them into the connection filter is a later step.
                      How to test this preview
                      1. Enable and approve the one-time VPN consent dialog.
                      2. Open doordash.com — it should fail to load; open wikipedia.org — it should load normally.
                      3. For app blocking, turn it on above and enable the FitShield accessibility service, then open a blocked app.
                      4. Disable to stop filtering. Uninstalling also stops it.

                      FitShield is free and local-first. If it has saved you an order or two, a coffee is always appreciated — never required.
