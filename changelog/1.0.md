# 1.0 — Final release: Google Play compliance

*2026-11-25*

Google Play rejected FitShield 0.56 (versionCode 8) with one sentence: *"Use of
VpnService is not a permitted use case. Remove the relevant service element from
the app manifest, for all artifacts across all release tracks."* This release is
the answer, and it is the last feature release: FitShield now enters maintenance
mode.

## What was removed

The Android app no longer contains a `VpnService`, and nothing that existed only
to serve one:

- the VPN service and the userspace connection filter behind it;
- the boot receiver and the "protection was restored" notice, which only
  restarted that filter;
- the generated host-rules asset and the tool that produced it;
- the `INTERNET` permission — the app now cannot open a network connection at
  all — and `RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED`.

The remaining Android permissions are `FOREGROUND_SERVICE` and
`FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE` (the opt-in keep-alive), `POST_NOTIFICATIONS`,
and `SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW`, plus the opt-in AccessibilityService, which reads only
the foreground package name.

## What changes for you

- **Android no longer blocks websites.** It blocks food-delivery and fast-food
  **apps** only, through the AccessibilityService. To block the same brands'
  websites, use the FitShield extension in Chrome, Firefox or Firefox for
  Android; it is unchanged.
- On Android, the main "Enable FitShield" switch now controls app blocking.
- The site-blocking status, the always-allow domain list, custom URLs and the
  domain tester are gone from the Android app, because they only ever served the
  connection filter.
- The VPN entry and key icon disappear after updating from 0.56.

## What did not change

App blocking, schedules, the pause timer, categories, per-app allow, the opt-in
keep-alive, all 83 languages, settings export and import, and stored data. An
install over 0.56 keeps its preferences. The browser extension changes in version
number only.

## Housekeeping done on the way

- The Android package map gained its Sweetgreen record (`com.sweetgreen.android.app`);
  1,512 app packages are bundled and 36 brands remain `needs_review`.
- Stale documentation lines were corrected, and every document that described
  VPN, website, boot-restore, Private DNS or `INTERNET` behaviour on Android was
  rewritten to match. Earlier release notes were not rewritten: they were
  accurate when written.

## The catalog did not change

Stated rather than skipped, because release notes that go quiet about the catalog
are how a stale number survives. Everything below is what 0.57 shipped, recounted
from the data for this release:

**2,505 curated brands across 111 markets**, split 514 delivery platforms and
1,991 fast-food brands.

Every brand carries its real category from the blocklist: *Asian*, *Bakery*,
*Burger*, *Catering*, *Chicken*, *Coffee*, *Delivery*, *Dessert*, *Fast Casual*,
*Grocery*, *Korean Food*, *Meal Delivery*, *Meal Kit*, *Mexican*, *Pizza*,
*Restaurant*, *Restaurant Discovery*, *Sandwich*, *Seafood*, *Smoothie* and
*Tea* — the 21 categories the current data uses.

The four brands 0.56 re-checked by hand still interrupt in the browser extension,
and were re-checked again here rather than carried over on trust: **LINE MAN**,
**EatFit**, **Jumia Food** and **BHC Chicken**.

**88 alternatives** answer them — **46** full recipes and **42** quick fixes.
**68** are vegetarian or stricter and **20** of those vegan; **26** need no heat
at all, and **37** need no stove or oven.

No brand was added, removed or recategorized, and no alternative was edited. If
you had a site blocked in 0.57 by the extension, it is blocked in 1.0. On Android
the catalog is now used for app blocking only.

## Compatibility

Chrome and other Chromium browsers, Firefox, and Android. Nothing about storage,
backups or migrations changed: a 0.57 profile carries straight over. The browser
extension still has no telemetry, no accounts, no network calls and no cloud, and
the same three permissions — `storage`, `declarativeNetRequest`, `alarms`.

## What this release does not claim

- The accessibility app-block flow has not been re-run on a physical phone for
  this release; that needs a person with a device.
- Whether Google Play accepts 1.0 is the Play Console's decision. The steps for
  the owner are in [`docs/PLAY_1.0_RESUBMISSION.md`](../docs/PLAY_1.0_RESUBMISSION.md).

**Play versioning:** `versionName 1.0` ↔ `versionCode 9` (0.56 ↔ 8 was the
rejected build).
